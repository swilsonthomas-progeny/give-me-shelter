    How this is counted

    Categories

    Microshelter: tiny houses and pallet units, combined on the chart and listed separately in each site's details. Emergency housing: rooms or apartments in a building leased whole. RV safe lot: parking spaces.

    What counts

    Sites inside Seattle city limits. A unit counts from the day its site opens to residents and comes off the day it closes. On the project bars, a dashed outline means announced, stripes mean under construction, a solid bar means open, and a blurred end marks an approximate opening date.

    Goals

    Goals include projections the city makes about how many units will open by a date. Each goal is marked at its target and due date. An arrow runs to it from the day it was set, starting at our count on that day. Missed and previous goals stay on the chart so the changes are visible. A deadline given as a season, such as the end of summer, is marked on its last day.

    City counts

    When the city states how many units it has added, the figure appears in the full record beside our own count for that date. We show both even when they don't match.

    Program notes

    Each site says whether it is part of the mayor's new shelter initiative, an earlier project the city says it sped up, or neither, repeating what the city and operators say.

    Updates

    A daily job reads news, city and provider feeds and drafts changes. A person reviews every change before it publishes.
